Rules & Sources
  • Ships with 2 beacons; max 10 per mower.
  • Beacons use UWB; ≤45 m apart with clear line of sight to each other and the robot.
  • ≥80% of the map covered by 2+ beacons at once; ≤10% with no signal.
  • Keep each beacon >5 m from obstacles taller than 60 cm.
  • Stand beacons perpendicular / upright (~76 cm tall).
  • Station: flat lawn, no slope; 2 m clear left, right & front; grass ≤6 cm; power supply ≥30 cm up.
  • Moving a beacon after mapping needs a full remap.

How to use this optimizer

  1. Upload a plan or photo of your garden (optional but easier).
  2. Set scale: choose the Ref Line tool, click the two ends of something you know the length of, type that length, hit Set Scale.
  3. Trace the lawn with Lawn Boundary (the area to mow). Add Obstacle >60cm polygons for trees, sheds, raised beds — these block signal and are excluded from the mowable area.
  4. Place the Station where your charger sits (it needs flat ground and 2 m clear on the sides & front).
  5. Compute Optimal Placement. Beacons are positioned to satisfy Ecovacs' real spec.

Why "2+ beacon coverage" matters

The GOAT G1 finds itself by triangulating off the UWB beacons, so it needs to "see" at least two beacons at any spot. Ecovacs requires ≥80% of the map covered by 2+ beacons and ≤10% with no coverage at all. Covering an area with a single beacon is not enough — that's the main thing this tool optimizes for, and the thing most DIY placements get wrong.

Line of sight

Tall obstacles (>60 cm) block the radio signal. The heatmap and validator both account for obstacles sitting between a beacon and a patch of lawn, and between beacons themselves.

This tool uses the 45 m beacon spacing limit as the beacon's effective coverage radius — a modeling assumption, since Ecovacs specifies the spacing but not a separate robot‑to‑beacon range, so the heatmap is approximate near the 45 m fringe. Also draw the house, fences and sheds as obstacles even when they sit outside the mow area: obstacles block line‑of‑sight wherever they are, and the optimizer only places beacons on mowable lawn (real installs often stake beacons just outside the edge).
